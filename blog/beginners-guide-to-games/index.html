Beginner's Guide to Royal X Casino Games: Slots, Teen Patti & Roulette

Here's exactly how each one works and a few simple tips to get you started on the right foot.

Slots: The Easiest Place to Start

Slots require no strategy, which is exactly why most beginners start here. Pick a slot title from the lobby, choose your bet size, and tap spin - matching symbols across a payline pay out automatically based on the game's paytable. Every slot on Royal X Casino shows its own paytable and RTP (Return to Player) percentage before you play, so it's worth checking that screen first. Beginner tip: start with a small bet size until you're familiar with a game's paytable and bonus rounds, then increase your stake once you understand how it pays.

Teen Patti: Pakistan's Favorite Card Game

Teen Patti is played with a standard 52-card deck, and each player is dealt three cards. The goal is simple: have the best three-card hand at the table, ranked from a Trail (three of a kind, the strongest hand) down to a Pure Sequence, Sequence, Color, Pair, and finally High Card. Players take turns betting blind (without looking at their cards, for a lower stake) or seen (after viewing their hand, for a higher stake). Beginner tip: play seen until you're comfortable reading how other players bet - blind play is a more advanced, higher-variance style worth trying once you know the hand rankings by heart.

Roulette: Simple Odds, Real Excitement

Roulette is a spinning wheel numbered 0-36, alternating red and black. You place chips on the table before the spin - on a single number for the biggest payout, or on broader options like red/black, odd/even, or a dozen of numbers for safer, smaller payouts. Once betting closes, the wheel spins and a ball lands on the winning number. Beginner tip: outside bets like red/black or odd/even pay close to even money and land far more often than a single-number bet, making them the easiest way to learn the table before chasing bigger payouts.

General Tips for Your First Week

Set a session budget before you start playing and stop when you reach it, win or lose.
