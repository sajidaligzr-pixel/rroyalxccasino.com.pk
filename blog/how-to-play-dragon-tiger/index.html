How to Play Dragon Tiger on Royal X Casino: Rules & Strategy

September 20, 2026  ·  4 min read

Dragon Tiger landed in the Royal X Casino lobby with the v2.67 update, and it's quickly become a favorite for players who want fast rounds without a lot of rules to learn. Here's everything you need to know to start playing.

The Basic Rules

Dragon Tiger is played with a single deck of cards. Each round, one card is dealt to the "Dragon" position and one card is dealt to the "Tiger" position. Whichever card is higher wins the round - that's the entire game. Aces are low, Kings are high. There's no hitting, no drawing, no decisions to make once you've placed your bet.

Placing Your Bet

Before the cards are dealt, you choose one of three bets: Dragon, Tiger, or Tie. Betting on Dragon or Tiger pays close to even money if that side wins. Betting on Tie pays much higher, but ties are rare, so most experienced players stick to Dragon or Tiger bets rather than chasing the bigger Tie payout.

Simple Strategy Tips

Since there's no skill involved in how the cards land, strategy in Dragon Tiger comes down to bankroll management rather than reading the game:

  • Stick to Dragon or Tiger bets - the Tie bet has a much higher house edge despite the bigger payout.
  • Set a round limit or budget before you start, since rounds move quickly and it's easy to play more than planned.
  • Some players track recent results to look for streaks, but each round is independent - past results don't affect the next card.
  • Start with small bets until you're comfortable with the pace of the game.

Why Players Like It

Dragon Tiger's appeal is its speed. A full round takes just seconds, so if you want a lot of action in a short session, it's one of the quickest games in the lobby. It's also a good pick for players who want something simpler than Teen Patti or Poker, without needing to learn hand rankings or betting rounds.
